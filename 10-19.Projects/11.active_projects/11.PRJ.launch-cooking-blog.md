# Launch a Cooking Blog

**One-sentence goal:** Publish a recipe blog under the Brightline Studio brand as a lead-generation channel for our recipe-photography client work, with at least 10 posts live and basic email capture working by the target date.

## Why this project

Brightline Studio (a fictional small freelance photography and content studio, used here purely as a worked example) wants a low-cost way to show off recipe photography work to prospective food-brand clients. A blog with a handful of strong posts, each with clean photography and a simple newsletter signup, is cheaper to build than paid ads and doubles as a portfolio.
