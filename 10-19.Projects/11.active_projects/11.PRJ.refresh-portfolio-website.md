# Refresh the Studio Portfolio Website

**One-sentence goal:** Rebuild Brightline Studio's portfolio website with a stronger case-study format and updated pricing page, ready to send to Ferncrest Kitchen Co. before their February vendor decision.

## Why this project

Brightline Studio (the same fictional freelance photography/content studio used throughout this template) has an inbound lead from Ferncrest Kitchen Co., a fictional regional food brand evaluating photography studios for their new product line. Their current portfolio site is two years old, doesn't show any food photography work, and has no clear pricing page — all three are likely to cost the pitch. This project exists to fix that before the client's stated decision window closes.
