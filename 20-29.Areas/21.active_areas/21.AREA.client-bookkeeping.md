# Client Bookkeeping

**Standard to maintain:** Every client invoice is sent within 2 business days of finishing a job, every payment is logged the day it arrives, and the monthly reconciliation is done before the 5th of the following month. (Fictional example area for Brightline Studio, a fictional freelance studio — not a real business.)

- Send invoices — within 2 business days of finishing a job
- Log incoming payments — same day they arrive
- Monthly reconciliation — by the 5th of the following month
- Quarterly estimated-tax set-aside — move the agreed percentage of income to a separate savings account each quarter
