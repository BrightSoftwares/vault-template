# Ideas Inventory

**Standard to maintain:** every candidate business idea gets written down here the moment it occurs — before it's evaluated, before it's built — so "I have an idea" and "I'm building it" stay two different, visible states. This area's job is to hold ideas at the `None` revenue-path stage (see `[[30-39.Resources/31.business-os/31.RESOURCE.three-currencies-and-revenue-gate-guide]]`) until one earns the right to become a real `.PRJ.` note.

## Ongoing responsibilities

- Capture a new idea here within a day of having it — a one-line entry is enough; resist the urge to flesh it out immediately.
- Once a month, re-read the list and re-score anything that's changed (a competitor launched, a client asked for it unprompted, a cost assumption turned out wrong).
- When an idea is picked to actually build, move its row to "Picked" below and create its `.PRODUCT.` and `.PRJ.` notes the same session — an idea that's been "picked" for weeks with no project note isn't actually picked yet.
