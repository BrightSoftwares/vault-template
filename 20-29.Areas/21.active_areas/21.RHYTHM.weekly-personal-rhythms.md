# Weekly Personal Rhythms

*(Fictional example for the Brightline Studio worked example — the studio's owner keeps this list to protect a few non-negotiable habits regardless of how busy client work gets. These are secular, generic examples; swap in whatever actually matters to you.)*

## Rhythms I'm protecting

- Monday morning planning walk — 20 minutes, no phone, before opening email
- One fully offline evening per week (Wednesday) — no client Slack, no email
- Friday afternoon admin block — invoices, bookkeeping, inbox zero — protected from new client calls
- A short weekly check-in with a friend or family member, unrelated to work

## Why these

Client work expands to fill however much time is available for it. These four rhythms exist so that planning, rest, and relationships don't get quietly crowded out week after week by whatever project feels most urgent that day.
