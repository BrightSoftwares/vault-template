# Grow Brightline Studio Into a Full-Time Business (2026-2027)

## The North Star

By the end of 2027, Brightline Studio no longer depends on trading hours for client photography shoots to cover its owner's living costs — a mix of productized guides and recurring client relationships does that instead, and client photography becomes a choice made for the work itself, not a financial requirement.

## Waypoints

- **W1 — First productized revenue stream live.** At least one downloadable product (starting with the Styled Recipe Shot List) is actually purchasable and has made its first sale. Target: mid-2026.
- **W2 — Productized revenue reaches parity with one month of client work.** Combined product revenue in a single month matches what one typical client shoot brings in, proving the productized side isn't just pocket change. Target: early 2027.
- **W3 — Full transition option exists.** Productized + recurring revenue alone would cover baseline living costs, making client photography fully optional rather than required. Target: end of 2027.
