# Portfolio Site Content Checklist

A standing checklist Brightline Studio (this template's fictional example business) uses whenever auditing or rebuilding its own portfolio website — reused across projects instead of rewritten each time. Any freelance or small-studio business could adapt this same shape to its own portfolio site.

## The checklist

- [ ] Homepage leads with the strongest, most relevant work — not the most recent work, unless they're the same thing
- [ ] At least one case study per service line offered, not just a grid of images with no context
- [ ] Pricing is visible somewhere, even as ranges or package tiers — "contact for quote" alone loses price-sensitive prospects before a conversation even starts
- [ ] At least two client testimonials, ideally tied to the case studies they reference
- [ ] Contact method is one click away from every page, not buried in a footer
- [ ] Mobile view checked last — most inbound traffic to a portfolio site arrives from a shared link on a phone, not a desktop bookmark

## Notes

- This checklist is deliberately generic — audit against it, then adapt case-study content per client type (a food brand cares about different things than a wedding client would).
