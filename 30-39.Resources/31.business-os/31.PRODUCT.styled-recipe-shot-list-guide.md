# The Styled Recipe Shot List

## What it is

A short downloadable PDF guide: the exact shot list, prop checklist, and natural-light setup Brightline Studio uses on every recipe photo shoot — written so a food blogger with a phone camera and a kitchen table can follow it themselves.

## Who it's for

Home-cooking food bloggers and small food-brand owners who shoot their own recipe photos, know the results look "almost right" but not professional, and can't yet justify hiring a photographer for every post.

## Value proposition

A full client photo shoot with Brightline Studio costs far more than a hobbyist blogger's photography budget. This guide sells the *method* instead of the *shoot* — the studio's actual working shot list, not a generic "photography tips" article — at a price close to what one recipe post's ad spend would cost.

## Price

€19 launch price, sold as a one-time downloadable purchase. Reasoned the same way as this repository's own modules (see `[[30-39.Resources/31.business-os/31.RESOURCE.three-currencies-and-revenue-gate-guide]]`): priced near the low end of what a hobbyist blogger would spend on a single boosted social post, since that's the realistic alternative use of their money.
