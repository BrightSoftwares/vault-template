# Template: Meeting Note

> A record of a single conversation — a client call, a check-in, a planning session — captured once and linked from wherever it's relevant, rather than retyped into a project note.

## What this template is for

Use this any time you have a conversation worth a written record: what was discussed, what was decided, and what happens next. The meeting note itself is a permanent, unchanging record of that conversation; any resulting work goes into the relevant Project or Area's `## Todo` section as its own task, linked back to the meeting note for context.

## Required frontmatter fields

```yaml
---
Type: "Meeting Note"
Date: "<YYYY-MM-DD>"
With: "<who this meeting was with>"
---
```

## Required sections

1. `## What we discussed` — a brief summary, not a transcript. Capture the substance, not every sentence.
2. `## Decisions made` — anything that was actually decided, even small things ("agreed to push the deadline a week").
3. `## Action items` — who does what by when. Each action item that's yours goes into the relevant Project or Area's Todo section too (with a link back here) — don't let action items live *only* inside a meeting note, or they'll get buried the next time you're not looking at it.
4. `## Follow-up needed` — anything left open or unclear that needs a future conversation.

## Notes on using this template

- **Write it during or right after the meeting**, not the next day from memory — decisions and action items are the two things that degrade fastest.
- Meeting notes live in their own `Meeting_notes/` folder at the vault root (dated-not-numbered, same exception pattern as Daily notes and Weekly reviews), named `YYYY-MM-DD-short-description.md` so they sort chronologically and stay searchable by date.
- If a meeting produces action items for more than one Project or Area, that's fine — link the meeting note from each of them rather than duplicating the note itself.
- Resist turning this into a full transcript. If you need a verbatim record for legal or compliance reasons, that's a different kind of document than this template is built for.
