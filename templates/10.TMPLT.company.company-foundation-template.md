# Template: Company Foundation

> A `.COMPANY.` note captures the handful of things about your business that are true no matter which specific project you're working on this week — its mission, its values, who it's for. Part of the **Business OS** module. Write it once, reference it from every project instead of re-explaining it each time.

## What this template is for

Every business-related `.PRJ.` note ends up needing to answer "why does this exist, and for whom?" Writing that answer fresh in every project note either gets skipped (so projects drift from each other) or copy-pasted (so it goes stale in twelve places at once). A single `.COMPANY.` note is the one place this gets written down, and every project links to it instead.

If you're a solo operator, one `.COMPANY.` note is usually enough for your entire business — you don't need this to feel like corporate paperwork; a few honest sentences per section is the goal, not a formal mission statement.

## Required frontmatter fields

```yaml
---
Type: "Company"
---
```

## Required sections

1. `## Mission` — one or two sentences: what does this business actually do, for whom, and why does that matter to the person paying for it?
2. `## Values` — 3–5 short principles that would actually change a decision (not generic virtues everyone would claim) — things you'd point to when explaining why you turned down a job or a shortcut.
3. `## Who this is for` — the kind of customer this business serves well, described concretely enough that you could recognize one in a conversation.
4. `## Notes` — anything else worth keeping close: founding story, a hard lesson, a boundary you've decided not to cross.

## Notes on using this template

- **Write it once, reference it everywhere.** Link to `[[31.COMPANY.brightline-studio-foundation#Mission]]` from a project's "Why this project" section instead of restating the mission in your own words each time — if the mission changes, you only update it in one place.
- If your business genuinely has more than one distinct company (rare for a solo side business, common if you later formalize a separate legal entity for a different venture), create a second `.COMPANY.` note rather than mixing two missions into one file.
- This is a foundation note, not a plan — it changes rarely. If you find yourself editing it every week, what you're actually writing is probably a `.GOAL.` note instead.
