# Template: Brand Identity

> A `.BRAND.` note captures how your business *sounds and looks* — voice, tone, and the handful of visual choices worth writing down once. Part of the **Business OS** module. A solo operator can have one company and one brand that are effectively the same thing; this note still earns its keep by keeping your voice consistent across every piece of content you write.

## What this template is for

Without a written-down voice, every blog post, email, and product description ends up sounding like whatever mood you were in that evening — which reads as inconsistent to a reader who sees more than one of them. A `.BRAND.` note fixes the voice once so any future writing (yours, or an AI assistant's) can match it instead of guessing.

## Required frontmatter fields

```yaml
---
Type: "Brand"
Parent Company: "[[<company-file>]]"
---
```

## Required sections

1. `## Voice` — 3–5 adjectives that describe how this brand talks (e.g. "direct, warm, a little dry"), each with one sentence of what it means in practice.
2. `## Tone do's and don'ts` — a short table or list: phrases/approaches that fit, and ones that don't, even if they'd otherwise be "good writing."
3. `## Visual notes` — color, type, or imagery choices worth keeping consistent, described in plain language (a full design system is a separate, heavier thing — this is just enough to keep casual content on-brand).
4. `## Sample line` — one or two sentences of actual copy in this voice, so "voice" isn't just adjectives — something to literally match tone against.

## Notes on using this template

- **Pull tone from here, not from invention.** When writing a blog post, an email, or a product page, read this note's `## Voice` and `## Sample line` sections first rather than deciding fresh each time what "sounds right."
- If your side business ever grows a second distinct offering with its own audience and voice (different enough that the same tone would feel wrong for both), that's the signal to create a second `.BRAND.` note under the same `.COMPANY.` note — most solo operators never need this, so don't create it preemptively.
- Keep this note short. A one-page brand voice guide gets read and used; a ten-page one gets written once and never opened again.
